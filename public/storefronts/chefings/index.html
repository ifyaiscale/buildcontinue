What ingredients can Chefings cut?

How do I clean and care for it?

Final cleaning and care instructions should match the production unit and packaging. The site is intentionally not inventing dishwasher-safe or disassembly instructions.

How much is standard shipping?

Standard shipping is included under the current launch policy. Taxes and the exact USD total are calculated at checkout.

Why can the checkout total differ from the display subtotal?
