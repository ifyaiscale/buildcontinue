PERSONALIZATION 101

Before you upload.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, front-facing image with the face visible and enough resolution for clean personalization.

Is checkout live yet?

Not for FaceJamas. Product selection and cart behavior are built, but payment remains locked until private customer-photo storage is connected to fulfillment.

Why is that extra step necessary?

A real personalized order must carry the correct private artwork reference into fulfillment. A browser-only preview is not sufficient.
